Import, preview, and submit a survey project

Radiacode is welcome but not required. Upload GPS-linked data from any Geiger counter, scintillation detector, spectrometer, drone, vehicle, walking survey, or community research project.

Preview supports CSV/TXT with latitude and longitude columns, GeoJSON/JSON, and GPX. The public map will display only approved projects.

Your project remains private until it is reviewed and approved.

How to interpret survey data

These are screening and research measurements, not regulatory determinations. Results depend on detector model, detector height, speed, dwell time, calibration, weather, geometry, and local background. Projects collected under different methods should not be treated as directly interchangeable.
